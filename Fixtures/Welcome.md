# A little room to read

A quiet place for your words. Open a Markdown document and make yourself comfortable.

## Made for the page

Folio brings research, meeting notes and technical documents into one calm reading view. **Write directly on the page, and Save when you are ready.**

> Good typography gives the words enough room to do their work.

### A few useful details

- Use **Contents** to find your way through a long document.
- Press **⌘F** to find a word or phrase.
- Adjust the text size to suit your screen.

| Document | A useful starting point |
| :--- | :--- |
| Research | Findings, evidence and footnotes |
| Meeting notes | Decisions and next steps |
| Technical guide | Clear instructions and exact code |

## Words, not workspaces

There is no library to build or account to create. Choose a file and start reading.[^1]

- [x] Read locally, even offline
