Folio — a calm place to read, write and review Markdown.

1. Drag Folio into Applications.
2. If Folio is already installed, quit it and choose Replace.
3. Open Folio from Applications.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 14 or later.
This early preview is not Apple-notarized. macOS may block its first launch.
Only proceed if you trust this download. Apple provides an Open Anyway option
in System Settings > Privacy & Security after a blocked launch.

Your documents and saved preferences are separate from the app and remain when
replacing it. Updates are manual for now; there is no automatic updater.
