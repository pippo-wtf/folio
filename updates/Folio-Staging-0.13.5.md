# Read for your agent.

Read what your agent wrote. Mark what matters. Comment on what needs to change.

- Select text and choose **Comment…** directly from the floating menu.
- Click an existing highlight to add or edit its comment.
- Use **Export Feedback…** to hand your agent structured feedback with exact edits, quoted passages and comments. Nothing is sent automatically.
