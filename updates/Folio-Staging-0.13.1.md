# A quiet heads-up when Folio gets better

- A small “Update available - check it out” link appears on the left of the toolbar when a newer release is confirmed.
- A thin divider separates the link from the Open icon.
- Background checks keep you on your page. Click the link to read what is new.
- Download and installation still require your clicks. Nothing installs automatically.

Staging retains the layout editor and presets.
