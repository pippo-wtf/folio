# A quieter reader. Clearer feedback.

- A simple comment field that follows Folio’s light and dark colors. Press Enter to save, or Shift+Enter for a new line.
- The filename stays on the left; writing and reading controls stay on the right.
- “App Update available. Check it out.” appears in your theme’s accent color, with a short letter-scramble reveal. Reduce Motion skips the animation.
- Fixed a window-closing path that could quit Folio while Finder was opening a Markdown file. Closing a window now keeps Folio running; use Quit Folio to exit.

Updates are announced only when available. Nothing downloads or installs until you choose to update.
